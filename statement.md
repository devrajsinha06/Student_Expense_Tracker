# Project Statement

## Project Title 
Student Expense Tracker

## Problem Statement
Students often spend money on food, travel, stationery and other daily needs but may not maintain a simple record of their spending. This project provides a small Python system to record expenses, manage a monthly budget and generate a spending report.

## Scope
The project covers:
- Expense entry
- Expense viewing and deletion
- Monthly budget management
- Monthly spending reports
- Local JSON data storage

The project does not include online banking, payment processing or cloud storage.

## Target Users
College students who want a simple personal expense management tool.

## High-Level Features
1. Expense management
2. Budget management 
3. Monthly reporting
4. Data validation 
5. Local data storage
6. Basic automated testing
